NeoTube is a modern, privacy-focused open-source Android video and music streaming client designed to give you complete control and an uninterrupted viewing experience.

Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, NeoTube delivers seamless playback without ads, tracking, or mandatory Google accounts.

Features:
• Ad-free playback: No video ads or sponsored interruptions.
• Background & Lock-Screen Audio: Listen to music or podcasts with the screen turned off.
• Picture-in-Picture (PiP): Multitask while watching your favorite videos.
• SponsorBlock: Automatically skip sponsored segments, intros, outros, and subscription reminders.
• Return YouTube Dislike: View authentic dislike counts on every video.
• DeArrow: Replaces sensationalized clickbait thumbnails and titles with community alternatives.
• Shorts Feed: Smooth vertical scrolling for short-form content with auto-scroll support.
• 100% On-Device Intelligence: Local recommendation engine that learns your interests privately without telemetry or server tracking.
• Offline Downloads: Download videos and audio directly to your device storage.
• Subtitles & Customization: Extensive subtitle formatting and dynamic Material You theme styles.
• NewPipe Import/Export: Easily migrate subscriptions and playlists without account lock-in.
